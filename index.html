The problem
For a small water system, running the plant is only half the job. The other half is proving you did it. The samples, the logs, the sanitary survey.

Today that record gets pieced together from memory at the end of a long day. A missed sample or a lost log becomes a violation, even when the water was perfect.

Monitoring & reporting is the No. 1 violation in the country.
About 1 in 5 water systems missed a monitoring or reporting requirement last year, and systems serving under 3,300 people carry most of it.
Source: EPA National Public Water Systems Compliance Report

Keepwell turns the work itself into the record, so nothing gets missed and nothing gets lost.
