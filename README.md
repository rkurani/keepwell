# Keepwell

**Do the round. Keep the record.**

Keepwell turns the field rounds a water utility already runs into the compliance record, the crew's know-how, and a map of every asset on the route. Captured hands-free through smart safety glasses and structured by AI. Know-how also comes in from sit-down interviews over the plans and from the utility's existing files (logbooks, O&M manuals, as-builts).

Prototype built with **Standard Water × The Water Center at Penn × Mentra**. Field footage is AI-simulated.

## Pages
- **`index.html`** — the product site (marketing). Five use cases: the record, know-how on the round, interviews, files, and the asset map.
- **`app.html`** — Keepwell Field: the work-order app. Each round shows the regulation it satisfies and the compliance record it generates.
- **`demo.html`** — the field-capture concept demo (operator + supervisor views, live capture, searchable library, asset telemetry).

Each page is fully self-contained (all media inlined as data URIs).

## Grounding
The compliance framing is grounded in real regulation (SDWA / 40 CFR Part 141, PA 25 Pa. Code Ch. 109). See **`REGULATORY_HOOKS.md`** for the cited hooks (sanitary surveys, RTCR, disinfectant residual, the 20 psi distribution floor, reporting deadlines, M/R violation stats, public-notification tiers, workflow → record map).

App flows are documented in **`FLOWS.md`**.
